Returns, refunds and cancellations

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy applies to orders placed on www.tradeindiamarketplace.com. Orders placed on Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho follow that marketplace's return policy.

Damaged, defective, wrong or missing items

  • Tell us within 48 hours of delivery by email or phone (details below), with your order number and photos (an unboxing video helps).
  • We arrange the pickup at no cost to you and send a replacement, or refund you in full if you prefer or if a replacement isn't available.
  • If a parcel is marked delivered but you didn't receive it, tell us within 48 hours and we will raise it with the courier.

Change of mind

  • You can return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unused, with all tags, accessories and original packaging.
  • The return shipping cost for change-of-mind returns is deducted from the refund. We tell you the amount before you confirm the return.
  • Some items can't be returned for hygiene or safety reasons (for example, opened food-contact items). Where this applies, it is stated on the product page.

Refunds

  • Refunds are issued within 7 business days after the returned item reaches us and is checked, or after we accept a missing-item claim.
  • Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID you give us.
  • Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show the credit.

Cancellations

  • You can cancel free of charge until the order is dispatched. Email or call us with your order number.
  • After dispatch, the order can't be cancelled, but you can return it under this policy.
  • If our supplier partner can't fulfil your order, we cancel it, tell you, and refund the full amount.
