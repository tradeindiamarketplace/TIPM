Shipping policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Where we deliver

We currently deliver within India only. Delivery is available to most PIN codes. Enter your PIN code at checkout to see whether we can deliver to you.

How your order is shipped

We sell products sourced from manufacturers and suppliers we have checked. To avoid extra handling, your order is packed and dispatched directly from the supplier partner's premises, with our invoice. If you order several items, they may arrive in separate parcels.

Dispatch and delivery times

  • We email you the dispatch date once our supplier partner confirms it, and the courier tracking number once your parcel is picked up.
  • After dispatch, delivery usually takes 2–7 business days depending on your PIN code. Remote areas can take longer.
  • If dispatch is delayed beyond the confirmed date, we tell you and you can cancel for a full refund.

Shipping charges

Any shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay. Product prices include GST.

Tracking

Use the tracking link in your dispatch email, or sign in to your account and open Orders.
