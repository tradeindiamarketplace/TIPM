Trade India Market Place sells useful home, kitchen and storage products sourced from Indian manufacturers and authorised suppliers. We don't run a warehouse. Your order ships straight from the supplier partner who makes or stocks it, packed with our invoice.

How we choose a supplier

Before any product is listed, we check the supplier:

  • business identity and an active GST registration
  • authorisation to sell the product (for branded goods)
  • evidence for quality claims, such as test reports or material certificates
  • dispatch capacity, packaging and tracking
  • how returns are handled

How we price

We count every cost: product, packing, shipping, returns and fees. Prices stay fair and the business stays sustainable. All prices include GST.

What we promise, and what we don't

  • We only give you a dispatch date once the supplier has confirmed it.
  • We only mark an order shipped once the courier has picked it up, and we send you the tracking number.
  • If something arrives damaged or wrong, tell us within 48 hours and we will put it right. See our refund policy.
