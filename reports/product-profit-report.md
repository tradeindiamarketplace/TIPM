# Product profit report (estimates, 2026-10-21)

> **Every number below is an ESTIMATE.** Supplier costs are public IndiaMART listing prices, not quotations. Marketplace fees come from third-party 2026 summaries that disagree with each other and are NOT verified on the official seller fee pages. Shipping, returns and advertising are assumptions. IndiaMART prices are usually bulk (MOQ) prices; a supplier who drop-ships single pieces will usually charge more, so these results are likely OPTIMISTIC. Use this to choose which products to get real quotes for, not to set prices.

Owner thresholds (config/settings.toml): minimum contribution **Rs 60/order**, minimum margin **12%**, maximum acquisition cost **Rs 80/order**. GST is treated as pass-through with input tax credit (needs GST registration).
